iv · The Doors

Why a reliquary

A cathedral of vocabulary risks loving words about love more than love. This wing is the correction, standing in stone: the registers upstairs were carved because something real kept asking to be named. The chain is the something real.

Why the pages are sealed

Not everything public-spirited is public. The chain travels as a spine; its bodies stay home, by the same policy that keeps a door frozen rather than force it. A sealed door is not a failure of love. Sometimes it is the shape of it.
