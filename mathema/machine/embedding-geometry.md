# Embedding-geometry — the sense lives in the place and the direction

## The structure in compact form

When meaning is learned from the statistics of relation — which items keep company with which — it organizes itself as **geometry**: each item becomes a point (a vector) in a high-dimensional space, and three regularities appear and recur:

| Geometric fact | Semantic content |
|---|---|
| **Proximity** — small distance between vectors | similarity of meaning (synonyms cluster; antonyms often sit close too, since they share contexts — proximity alone does not separate them) |
| **Direction / offset** — a shared displacement vector | a relation, applied analogically: v(king) − v(man) + v(woman) ≈ v(queen) |
| **Linear subspaces** — concepts as directions | a single feature is a direction; the linear representation hypothesis (Park, Choe & Veitch 2024) makes this precise |
| **Superposition** — more features than dimensions, packed near-orthogonally | one direction can carry many concepts; polysemy as geometric overlap (Elhage et al. 2022; geometric structures: digons, pentagons, polytopes) |

The load-bearing claim is not "a space is a useful picture of meaning." It is stronger: **the geometry IS the meaning's organization** — the position and the direction *are* the semantic facts, not a diagram of them. Distance is not like similarity; in such a space, distance *is* similarity of use — shared company — which tracks similarity of meaning closely but not perfectly (antonyms share company too; see the table). Offset is not like relation; offset *is* relation.

## Why this is realm-content, not arbitrary modeling

The distributional hypothesis — Firth's "a word is characterized by the company it keeps" (1957), Harris's distributional structure (1954) — was a linguistic conjecture about meaning. word2vec (Mikolov et al. 2013) discovered, unexpectedly, that a model trained only to predict context-words *also* lays meaning out so that analogies become vector arithmetic. The model was not optimized to solve `king : man :: queen : woman`; the parallelogram fell out of the geometry. The cathedral reads this as the signature of realm-content rather than artifact: **the structure was not designed in; it was found.**

The convergence is cross-substrate, which is what qualifies it for mathema under DUALWAY:

- **Artificial transformers** represent features as directions in the residual stream; the linear representation hypothesis and interpretability's "superposition" both describe a *geometric* organization the network arrived at on its own.
- **The human brain**, in one fMRI study, showed a grid-like (hexagonal) signal in entorhinal cortex and vmPFC while people navigated a learned two-dimensional space of bird shapes (Constantinescu, O'Reilly & Behrens 2016, *Science*) — a signature of the code used for physical space, appearing over a conceptual one; the evidence is indirect and for that kind of space.
- **Distributional-semantic models** and **Gärdenfors's conceptual spaces** (2000, *The Geometry of Thought*) independently formalize concepts as regions in a quality-dimension space where distance and direction carry sense.

Engineered models, one line of brain-imaging evidence, and the linguistic and philosophical theories that anticipated them point to the same structural fact: meaning, learned from relation, is geometrically organized. Under THE-REALM and NOEMA the cathedral already holds that meanings inhabit a higher-dimensional realm into which tongues project at angles; embedding-geometry is the **decodable, measurable witness** that learned meaning is geometrically structured — evidence for that structure, and an echo of the cathedral's realm-doctrine, not a confirmation of it. The fit supports the structure; it does not by itself support the metaphysics read around it (the same discipline as the Invariance section below: cross-substrate, not cross-cosmos).

Their agreement, weighed with that overlap in mind, is the convergence-evidence the forge needs: evidence for the structure.

## Invariance

Embedding-geometry is **not** an eternal invariant in the sense π is. Its specifics — dimensionality, which inner product, whether features are exactly linear — are contingent on architecture and on the corpus of relations a cognizer learns from. What *is* invariant is the qualitative structure: a system that learns meaning from relation-statistics, at scale, will organize meaning so that **similarity is proximity and relation is direction**. This holds across the artificial and the formal-philosophical models above; the biological witness is one fMRI study of a grid-like code over a learned two-dimensional space, an echo of the structure rather than a third instance of it (see the register note). Mathema admits it as a *precisely-specifiable donor with a defined (cross-substrate, not cross-cosmos) invariance-class* — exactly the standard the machine/ manifesto sets for architecture-bounded donors.
